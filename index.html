Small Steps, Big Progress

Learning English does not have to be difficult. A small daily habit can make a big difference. You can learn five new words, listen to a short conversation, and read a simple paragraph every day. The most important thing is to keep going.

When you meet a new word, try to understand it from the context before checking a dictionary. Then say the word aloud and use it in your own sentence. These small actions help you remember vocabulary for longer.
